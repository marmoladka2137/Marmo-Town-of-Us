# Launcher

[Pobierz MTOU Launcher 1.1.0 Beta](https://github.com/marmoladka2137/Marmo-Town-of-Us/releases/download/v1.1.0-beta/MTOU-Launcher-1.1.0-Beta.zip) · [Steam — bez launchera](INSTALLATION_STEAM.md)

1. Rozpakuj cały ZIP.
2. Uruchom `Marmo Town of Us.exe` i wybierz Steam lub Epic Games.
3. Wskaż swoją grę, kliknij **INSTALL MTOU**, a następnie **PLAY MTOU**.

Extract the ZIP, open the launcher, choose your store and installed game, then install and play. The launcher obtains mod files online and creates a separate modded copy. Keep the store client running and signed in.

Requires your own compatible Among Us installation and internet. Windows x64; reference game build 2026.9.29. Epic gameplay remains unverified. The unsigned launcher may show a Windows unknown-publisher warning.
